YouthOpps is an independent, nonprofit-oriented platform that helps people discover opportunities published by external organizations.

Opportunity records in this repository are collected from independent third-party sources. Copyright, database rights, trademarks, and any other applicable rights remain with their respective rights holders.

YouthOpps does not claim ownership of third-party publisher content. Unless explicitly stated by the relevant rights holder, YouthOpps does not grant a license to reproduce, redistribute, republish, or commercially exploit that content.

Public availability of this repository does not mean its contents are in the public domain, or that all records are available under an open data license. The Apache License 2.0 used for YouthOpps software does not apply to third-party opportunity data in this repository.

## Attribution and reuse

Records should retain attribution and a link to their original publisher whenever available. Users wishing to reuse records must independently check the relevant publisher's license, terms, permissions, and applicable law.

Some factual information may not be protected by copyright, but legal rights and reuse restrictions vary by jurisdiction and dataset. This notice does not create additional rights or restrictions.

## Corrections and removal

Publishers and rights holders may request attribution corrections, data corrections, or removal by emailing **contact@youthopps.org**. Please identify the source and affected URLs so the request can be reviewed.
